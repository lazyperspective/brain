The Red Gathers

A first-frame / last-frame film made entirely in code. It opens on one painting and ends on another; the fifteen seconds between them are computed in the browser as they play. A wind takes the blossom apart petal by petal, the traveller walks out through the gate, the colour drains from the world until only red is left, and all of the red gathers into the sun over the sea where he sits down to rest.

The in-between is built only from the two keyframes' own pixels and from procedural drawing: petals and ink flakes are Voronoi pieces cut from the paintings, the cliff and the house are assembled from the last frame's dark mask, and the traveller, his hat, the wind and the water's motion are drawn in code.

Serve this folder over http (for example python3 -m http.server) — browsers will not let a page read the pixels of images opened from file://. Keyframe images go in assets/ as first.jpg and last.jpg.
