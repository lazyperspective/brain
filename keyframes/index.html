Keyframes

01 · The Red Gathers. A first-frame / last-frame film made entirely in code. It opens on one painting and ends on another; the fifteen seconds between them are computed in the browser as they play. A wind takes the blossom apart petal by petal, the traveller walks out through the gate, the colour drains from the world until only red is left, and all of the red gathers into the sun over the sea where he sits down to rest.

02 · The Evening Walk. The same reaper, from a flat khaki noon to an orange evening. The wind rises; he lifts his head, lifts the scythe off the ground, turns and walks. He takes a staff that stands planted at the brink of the plain and walks down into a valley of red flowers as the sun goes down, swings the scythe up over his shoulder in one long arc, and slows on the slope as two birds cross the sky, settling into the last frame's pose.

The in-between is built only from the two keyframes' own pixels and from procedural drawing. In 01, petals and ink flakes are Voronoi pieces cut from the paintings, the cliff and the house are assembled from the last frame's dark mask, and the traveller, his hat, the wind and the water's motion are drawn in code. In 02, the reaper is an articulated cut-out puppet made from each keyframe's own figure — hood, cloak, arm, staff and scythe as separate meshes — with a simulated cloth for his tatters and cape, procedural legs that plant their feet, and a field grown from flower sprigs cut out of the last frame.

Serve this folder over http (for example python3 -m http.server) — browsers will not let a page read the pixels of images opened from file://. Keyframe images go in assets/ as first.jpg / last.jpg (film 01) and evening-first.jpg / evening-last.jpg (film 02).
