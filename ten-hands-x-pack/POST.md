# Ten Hands — post kit

## Post options

**Short**
> I asked for ten artworks, each by a different imagined artist, drawn entirely in JavaScript. No images, no models, no textures: every pixel is computed when you open the page.
> Yarn painting, blind emboss, mezzotint, a one-line drawing, a medieval manuscript, a kintsugi bowl, a woodblock print, a ruined fresco, a charcoal animation and a cut-paper whale.

**Thread opener**
> Ten artworks. Ten imagined artists. Zero image files.
> Every one of these is drawn by code at the moment you open it. 🧵

**Thread, one post per work** (pair with the matching image or clip)
1. A Huichol-style yarn painting. Every strand is laid by code, twisted and fuzzy. Zoom in and it turns into thread.
2. White paper pressed into white paper. Vole tracks wander across the snow and stop at the print of an owl's wings. Move the light to see it.
3. An imaginary prison after Piranesi, ray-marched in 3D, then "printed" as a mezzotint: velvet black, scraped back to light.
4. One line of ink that never lifts. It becomes a cup, a whale, a tightrope walker, Mount Fuji, and finally the hand that is drawing it.
5. A medieval Book of Hours where the margins took over. A knight surrenders to a snail. The gold glints as you move.
6. One tea bowl, broken and mended twice with gold. Drag to turn it; the oil spots shift colour as it moves.
7. A 1930s-style woodblock print of vending machines in the snow. Every visit prints a new impression.
8. A Roman garden fresco after two thousand years. Where the paint fell off, the red underdrawing waits underneath.
9. One hill, one century, one sheet of charcoal paper. Everything erased stays behind as a ghost.
10. A cut-paper tunnel book. Look through a whale's mouth, nine layers deep, into a room where someone reads by lamplight.

**Closer**
> Each one has its own technique: distance fields, ray marching, simulated paper, simulated printing, simulated decay. All of it plain JavaScript, Canvas and WebGL. No libraries.

- 01: A square yarn painting in vivid colours on a dark ground: a blue deer leaping, antlers turning into maize, a sun with a face, a green serpent, a small figure in a feathered hat.
- 02: A sheet of white paper with no ink. Raking light reveals tiny embossed animal tracks leading to the imprint of an owl's spread wings in snow.
- 03: A dark mezzotint print of a vast vaulted prison with arches, a bridge, a great wooden wheel, a hanging lantern and a shaft of light.

- 05: A medieval manuscript page with Gothic script, a gold initial with a sleeping monk and a cat, and marginal drawings including a knight kneeling before a giant snail.
- 06: A black tea bowl on a dark surface, with iridescent spots inside and cracks repaired in gold.
- 07: A Japanese-style woodblock print of a snowy night: a figure with a red umbrella waits by three glowing vending machines while a black cat watches.
- 08: A broken fragment of a Roman fresco showing a garden with fruit trees, a jay, a lattice fence and poppies; large patches of plaster are missing, revealing red underdrawing.
- 09: A charcoal drawing of a hill with a lone tree, layered with grey ghost traces of earlier drawings: pylons, houses, a road.
- 10: A cut-paper diorama in deep blues: the open mouth of a whale with layers of ribs, leading to a small lamplit room where an old woman reads.
