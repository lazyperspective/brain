## How it works
- **One three.js scene builder for both styles** (`world.js` + `arch.js`, `nature.js`, `people.js`, `rides.js`, `shots.js`). It starts from `plan/greybox.js` (terrain, paving polygons, kerbs, track sweeps, supports) with a material hook, then hides the grey-box buildings, guests, trees, props, trains, boats, queues, the wooden coaster and the hero flat rides and replaces them with detailed instanced models. Everything is placed from `park.json` (nothing moved, added or removed). Cameras always come from `park.camera(shot, t)`, ride clocks from `park.trainFronts` / `rideTime` / `flatRide` with each shot's overrides, guests from `park.guestsAt(t)`. The sun is SW (az 225°, el 38°) `#FFF1DC` with a `#BFD9FF` hemisphere fill, and shadows fall NE.
- **Harness**: `run.js` serves `film/` over http, drives `page.html`/`main.js` in headless Chromium (`chromium.launch()` with no options, SwiftShader WebGL2) and pipes frames straight into `nice ffmpeg -threads 2`. No PNG sequences go to disk. Usage: `node run.js --shot 07` (full) or `--stills 0,40,80 --outdir dir` (review). Debug flags: `--raw` (no post), `--ns` (no shadows), `--hide group`, `--prof` (triangle budget per group). `check.sh <id>` runs ffprobe and extracts the cut frames.
- **Gouache** (`post.js`, `mats.js`): Lambert materials with world-space pigment mottling and an illustrative light model (warm lit / cool-violet shadow bands). The post pipeline runs a hand wobble and a generalised Kuwahara (half res, mixed back with full-res detail), then **brush dabs** (each 11 px screen cell carries one elongated, bristled stroke painted with the colour at its centre, blended only into similar colours so shapes keep their edges), pigment pooling at colour edges, a soft cool outline at depth breaks, paper fibre/granulation, a warm grade and a vignette. The painted sky dome has fbm cumulus lit from the SW and distant blue hills. Outside the berm are painted patchwork fields with hedgerows.
- **Pixel tycoon**: the same scene renders at a native 480×270 with toon (3-tone) materials and BasicShadowMap. The camera is snapped to the native pixel grid (at most ½ px off), and a depth-silhouette ink outline is added. The result is quantised to a 66-colour palette (the prototype palette plus the park key colours) with 4×4 Bayer dithering between the two nearest colours. It is upscaled ×4 nearest-neighbour by ffmpeg. The grass has mown stripes and dithered tufts, and a solid red ribbon under the hero track makes the coaster read at native size.

## Detail added (what the camera sees)
- **Guests**: 4 body variants (trousers, dress with long hair, shorts with sun hat, jeans with backpack and cap) with skin, hair, shoes and pants from palettes, and shirts from `clothes_palette`. Legs and arms swing in the vertex shader. Children are scaled to 0.62. There are strollers, held balloons on strings, balloon sellers, cart vendors, the bandstand band and queued guests. Distance LOD uses a ~400-tri body near the camera and a ~110-tri body far away, with frustum culling, and guests within 4 m of a low camera are hidden.
- **Main Street**: forced-perspective storeys from `storey_heights`. Each façade has pilasters, storey bands, a dentilled cornice, framed and mullioned windows (sills, lintels or pediments, shutters, window boxes with flowers), shopfronts per type (display windows with goods, double doors, arcades, bay windows), blank fascia signs, striped or scalloped or plain awnings with valances, and roofs per type (flat cornice with pediment, hipped with chimney, mansard with dormers and finials, gable-front with oculus and bargeboards, corner turret with striped onion dome). There are rooftop flags, plus bunting pennants and string lights from the plan.
- **Entrance**: brick towers with stone quoins, arched windows, copper domes and flags. The wrought-iron lattice arch has bulbs and a blank cartouche. The ticket kiosks have arched windows, red/cream striped canopies and finials. The turnstiles have tripod arms under a striped canopy. Entrance and town-square fountains have animated jets, and the hub moat has its 24 jets. The riveted girder railway bridge sits on brick abutments with an arched portal and ivy. The tank loco and 5 open coaches carry riders. The car park has 1,250 modelled cars, a road-train tram, the drop-off canopy and lamp poles.
- **Paths**: world-space paving shaders per type (herringbone brick, radial setts with a compass ring, concrete grid with brick bands, boardwalk planks, flagstones, packed earth, stamped pastel). Lamp posts come in all 8 styles, plus hanging baskets, benches, bins, urn planters, drinking fountains, umbrella tables with chairs, carts, signposts with blank blades, mesh ride fences and the white lake railing. Queues have posts, double rails and striped canopies.
- **Rides**: the hero lift has catwalks, handrails, a chain, an anti-rollback rack and bulbs on the lift and loop. There are footers, lattice-tower bracing, a sawtooth station, and trains with riders (some with arms up) and a nose cone. The wooden coaster has a laminated stack, steel running rails, walkboards, handrails, ties, bents with ledgers every 2.5 m, X-braces, stringers and longitudinal diagonals. The Ferris wheel has twin rims, a lattice ring, A-frame legs, 30 open gondolas with riders and 720 bulbs. The swing ride has a striped canopy, bulbs and chairs on chains with riders. The pirate ship has an extruded hull, stripe, seats, mast and sail. The observation tower has bands, a gold crown and a glass cabin with riders. The drop tower, the carousel (striped canopy, rounding board, mirrors, horses with poles), swan pedal boats with riders, flume logs and rapids rafts are also modelled.
- **Nature**: species-shaped clustered crowns (oak, plane, clipped lime, cloud-crown pine, tiered fir, palm fronds, banana, layered jungle broadleaf, birch, cherry, cypress, willow with hanging strands, red maple) with per-tile hi/lo LOD by camera distance, shrub zones with flowers, and topiary animals.

## Shot specifics
- **02**: continues 01's PATH_ARRIVAL exactly (same keys). The railway override puts the train on the bridge as we pass under at 7.0, and the whip left comes from the camera keys.
- **05**: build mode. The wooden coaster is laid from s=0 to 612 m over 16.0–18.5 (pieces beyond the head are hidden and the last 8 m drop in from above). There is a flashing yellow/orange construction diamond, a guide post, a bobbing arrow cursor and sparkles at the build head. Train 0 runs on the shot's ride clock behind the head, and train 1 appears once the circuit closes. Frontier buildings and the water tower pop with squash and stretch plus dust puffs (16.5 / 16.75 / 17.0 / 17.25), and the frontier pines pop on 16th notes from 17.5. Guests fast-forward from 18.0, easing to walking pace by 19.0, and frontier guests stream in. The midway is an empty dirt lot with stake flags, and its rides, booths, food court, hedges, props and guests are not built. The toy-box crate (70×46×30 at (120,0,62), red lid band, stars, latch, hinges on the north edge) drops in at 16.5 with dust. The snap zooms come from the camera keys. At 16.0 the frame lines up with 04's last frame (04 also shows midway and frontier as an unbuilt blueprint there).
- **07**: arrival map t = 24.5 + 0.55·d(lighthouse)/50 m + 1.9·nearness(camera) + fbm. It gives swings ≈25.0, wheel ≈25.5, ship ≈25.85 and the camera foreground ≈27.0. Before the wave arrives the scene is desaturated grey-green with rust tint, drifting low mist and a grey sky, with overgrowth clumps and ivy on the wheel legs, swing mast and ship frame (they shrink away as the colour arrives). The lighthouse lamp ignites with a glow at 24.5. The swing ride spins up from 25.0, boats start at 25.5, wheel bulbs come on at 26.0, and the wheel turns and the ship swings from 26.5. Guests pop in just after the colour reaches them, and a warm light band rides the wave front.
- **09**: the POV uses the shot's ride clock. Our own lead car is a special model (no front-row riders, slim lap bars, low nose) so the camera is not inside geometry.
- **12**: ortho close on the loop at ×4. A twinkling sparkle trail follows the train.

## What I'm unhappy with
- The gouache is "illustrated 3D with paint": shapes are still geometric, the brush dabs are uniform in size, and close guests are simple doll figures (no faces).
- The 09 POV foreground (red car deck and lap bars) is blocky, and the park below is mostly out of frame on the lift (the camera is the director's).
- In 07 the transition band shows as a large bright and dark sweep across the lake mid-way. The lake has no reflections.
- In pixel at the widest zoom (ortho 120) the wooden-coaster lattice dithers into a busy texture, and the backstage area reads grey and dithered.
- Swing chairs and gondola canopies are simple, and the carousel horses are crude.
- Render speed: under contention from the other agents, gouache ran at ~10 s per frame and pixel at ~11 s per frame for the full-park wide frames. The cost is dominated by vertex load in SwiftShader, so I added tile culling, LOD and lighter rider meshes.
