# The edit: park montage (44.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, seamless loop)

One park, eight art styles. The signature move is to **keep the camera moving and change the material on the beat**. Every style renders the same `plan/park.json` from the same camera, `park.camera(shot, t)`, so a cut can change only the style while the motion carries straight through. Each style's transformation is used exactly once, as a big moment. The film builds to a coaster ride-along in which the style changes on every beat, then resolves into the dusk finale and the snow globe. The globe's last frame lands on the closed ticket that the film opens with.

- **Grid:** 120 BPM in 4/4. A beat is 0.5 s (15 frames) and a bar is 2 s (60 frames). The film is 22 bars, and every cut falls on a beat.
- **Loop:** the last frame of shot 16 is the first frame of shot 01: the same closed ticket, the same pose relative to the ticket, the same table and the same light. The score loops sample-accurately, with its reverb tails wrapped round.
- **Machine-readable edit:** `plan/shots.json`, generated by `gen/build_shots.py`. Each shot has its camera keys, beats, ride-clock overrides, transitions, focus, must-show elements and render frame counts.

## Climax ride clock (hero coaster)

Each climax shot overrides the ride clock as `rides.hero_coaster = {t0, tau0, rate}`, where `tau` is seconds into the track's time table.

| shot | film time | tau | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| lift | 28–30 | 44 → 52 | ×4 |
| crest approach | 30–31 | 53 → 56.6 | ×3.6 |
| crest turn | 31–32 | 59.3 → 63.5, drop starts exactly at 32.0 | ×4.2 |
| drop | 32–33 | 63.5 → 66.3 | ×2.8 |
| loop | 33–34 (exterior) | 75.6 → 79.6 | ×4 |
| loop | 34–35 (POV) | top of the loop at 34.5 | ×1.7 |
| corkscrew | 35–36 | 80.7 → 83.1 | ×2.4 |

Outside the climax, rides follow `animation.*.ride_time_at_film_t0` with global film time, so they stay continuous across every match cut.

## Rules every renderer must follow (so that cuts match)

1. **Camera:** always evaluate `park.camera(shot, t)` (park.js or park.py) at `t = render.t_start + i/30`. Don't invent moves. Use the same fov, aspect 16:9, and camera up (world up; POV uses the track's up).
   - Near and far planes: 0.3 / 6000 m.
   - Ortho shots: the visible height is `2 × orthoHalfHeight`, centred on `target`.
   - Pixel art renders the ortho camera at a native 480×270 grid scaled ×4 (nearest). Snap-zoom keys change the scale in steps.
2. **Content:** draw what is in `park.json`. Use the baked track frames, supports and bents, and the placed props, trees and guests (sub-sample guests in very wide shots if needed). Use the ride clocks from `park.rideTime(id, t, train, shot)`.
   - The style may add texture and wear, but it must not move, add or remove landmarks: the tower, coasters, wheel, carousel, lake, Main Street, gate or berm.
3. **Light and time of day:**
   - Day shots (02–14): sun from the **south-west** (azimuth 225°, elevation 38°, `lighting.sun_day`), warm white `#FFF1DC`, sky fill `#BFD9FF`. Shadows fall to the north-east.
   - 07 starts desaturated, grey and misty in the same light direction.
   - 15 goes from dusk to night, with the sun setting in the west. Night bulbs are warm `#FFC76B` and cool `#9FE3FF`.
   - 16 is night, inside a room lit by a warm lamp.
   - The tabletop sets (01 and the end of 16) share the warm key `#FFD9A0` from the upper-left-back, direction to the light `[-0.5, 0.8, -0.35]`.
4. **Colour keys:** use `lands[].palette`.
   - Hero coaster: signal red `#E23B2E` track with cream `#F4EBD9` supports. Wooden coaster: honey timber `#D9B98C` with a dark track `#6B4A2E`. Family coaster: teal `#2BB3A3` with yellow `#F5C542`.
   - Tower: white with a gold crown. Wheel: white, with gondolas in a rainbow. Carousel: red and cream. Main Street brick: `#B5563F`.
   - Each style may reinterpret the colours, but the hue identity must survive every cut.
5. **No text anywhere:** signs are blank, or carry icons such as a cross or a star.
6. **Output:**
   - Path: `film/shots/<id>.mp4`.
   - Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p, CRF ≤ 18. Exactly `render.frames` frames, starting at `t_start` (6 handle frames before the edit-in and 6 after the edit-out).
   - Shots on twos: render the unique frames and write each one twice, so the mp4 is still 30 fps.
   - Don't write frame PNGs to disk; pipe them into ffmpeg. Run one ffmpeg at a time.
7. **Motion blur and whips:** whip pans have the motion inside the camera keys. Add your own blur if your style supports it. The assembler adds a smear at whip cuts anyway.
8. **Sets:**
   - **Paper (01):** the map sheet covers x −300…300 and z −232…248, on a table at y=0. It has 3×5 panels of 200×96. The closed stack is on the centre panel, centred on (0, 8), with `ticket_print.png` on top.
   - **Toy box (06):** 70×46×30, centred on (120, 0, 62). The lid is hinged on the north edge. The clockwork box is at (176, 0, 84) and its key at (176, 4.5, 88.5).
   - **Globe (16):** the sphere is centred on (0, 120, −20) with r=400. The park is clipped to r=350 around (0, −20). The wooden base runs from y=0 down to the table at y=−160. The ticket stack (200×96×6) lies at (0, −157, 648) with the print up, oriented as in 01.

## Score (`audio/score.wav`, generated by `audio/score.py`)

It is fully synthesised at 48 kHz/24-bit and is exactly 44.000 s. It loops seamlessly: the tails wrap to the start and the reverb is a circular convolution. The master is **−14.0 LUFS integrated, −1.5 dBTP** (4× oversampled look-ahead limiter). The colour is a fairground band organ (calliope pipes with vibrato and breath), oom-pah tuba with accordion stabs, glockenspiel, music box, chiptune square and pads, over a punchy 4-on-the-floor kit. The key is C major.

## Assembly

Run `python3 film/assemble.py`, or `--placeholders` to fill missing shots with solid colour or the grey-box clips in `scratch/placeholders/`. It:
- trims each shot's handles off by frame (`trim=start_frame=6:end_frame=6+film_frames`), scales to 1920×1080 and concatenates;
- adds whip smears and the drop flash, then muxes `score.wav`;
- writes `out/park_film.mp4` (CRF 16, AAC 320k) and then `out/park_film_share.mp4` (CRF 23, maxrate 12M), one ffmpeg at a time.

It was tested with 4 grey-box shots plus colour placeholders. The output is 1320 frames and the cuts are frame-exact (frame 119 is the last frame of shot 01 and frame 120 the first of shot 02).
