# Drawings from Middle-earth

Four drawings of scenes from Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*, each in its own traditional medium, each made
entirely in JavaScript when the page opens. They are original interpretations of the books, not of any film.
Press and hold on any drawing for a magnifying lens.

| # | Drawing | Medium |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | **The Bridge of Khazad-dûm** | Pen and ink after Doré, with fire washes and white gouache: the wizard on the bridge, the Balrog in shadow and flame, snowy peaks and the distant Eye through a rift |
| 2 | **The Pillars of the Kings** | Graphite and watercolour: the Argonath towering over the Fellowship's three boats |
| 3 | **The Ride of the Rohirrim** | Red, black and white chalk on toned paper: Théoden leads the charge toward Minas Tirith at dawn |
| 4 | **Caras Galadhon by Lamplight** | Silver and gold ink on indigo: the city of the trees at night, with the Mirror of Galadriel below |

Open `index.html` (works from `file://`). Keys: <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd>, <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>4</kbd>, <kbd>i</kbd>, <kbd>h</kbd>.
