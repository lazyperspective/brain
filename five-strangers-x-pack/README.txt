All MP4s are silent. X accepts up to 4 images or 1 video per post.

About the videos: the wall clip is a real time-lapse of the piece's sunlight, rendered frame by frame.
The other clips are camera moves and crossfades over high-resolution renders (the colony's ants,
Mr. Pruett's blink and the poster's bulb don't move in them). A screen recording of the live gallery
on a normal computer will show the ants walking.
