# Five Strangers — post kit

## Post options

**Short**
> Five artworks by five imagined artists who never met, drawn entirely in JavaScript. No images, no models, no textures: every pixel is computed when the page opens.
> A faded wall. An ant colony. A teddy bear's X-ray. A melting plasticine man. A Ghanaian cinema poster.

**Thread opener**
> Five artworks. Five strangers. Zero image files.
> Each one is drawn by code the moment you open it, and each was made to astonish for a different reason. 🧵

**Thread, one post per work** (pair with the matching image or clip)
1. The simplest one. A wall painted deep madder long ago, bleached pink by forty years of sun, except where a portrait hung. One nail. An afternoon of sunlight crosses it in six minutes.
2. The most complex one. One square metre of meadow under a picnic, cut in section: an ant colony of several hundred walking their own tunnels, and everything the ground has kept. "Front door key, lost Thursday." Move the magnifying glass anywhere.
3. An X-ray of a 1950s teddy bear on a lightbox. Wood-wool, cotter pins, a voice box, an arm once restuffed with rags, and a ring sewn in where the heart would be. The conservator's note: "leave in situ."
4. Mr. Pruett was left on the radiator. He is perfectly fine, thank you. Plasticine, fingerprints and all, ray-marched in WebGL.
5. A mobile-cinema poster in the Ghanaian tradition, painted on stitched flour sacks for a film nobody has seen: MAMMY WAGON FROM HELL, PART 2. Every letter, brushstroke and sack thread drawn by code.

**Closer**
> Five techniques that share nothing: shader-lit plaster, gouache-style Canvas illustration with a living ant colony, volumetric X-ray projection, ray-marched clay, and procedural oil paint on sacking. Plain JavaScript, Canvas and WebGL. No libraries.
