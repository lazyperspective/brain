Prototype set · 7 loaders

Loader Menagerie

Seven playful loading animations, each a small creature or contraption that carries its own progress. Poke, drag and tap them. They load, celebrate and start over.

  • Poke click or tap
  • Drag bars and data bits
  • Keys Tab to a stage, Enter or Space

Glass Butterfly

glass · liquid

Frosted wings fill with colour as it loads. It drifts after your pointer; tap a flower or anywhere to give it a place to land.

Fluttering about

Pixel Snail

pixel art · slapstick

The slime trail is the progress bar. Hover to make it sweat and hurry; poke it and it hides in its shell, pausing the download.

Setting off

Jelly in a Jar

liquid · glass

The water level is the progress. Sweep across to rock the jar and slosh it; tap the glass to startle the jelly.

Waiting for water

Liquid Hourglass

liquid · glass

Amber drips through the neck and the percentage is etched in both bulbs. Click to flip it. It objects, then pours faster to catch up.

Dripping

Origami Crane

paper · structure

A sheet folds itself one diagram step at a time. Hover and the paper rustles loose; poke it and it crumples, then smooths itself out.

Colour side down

Pixel Cat vs. Progress Bar

pixel art · slapstick

The cat keeps nudging the bar toward the edge. Drag it back to safety, or tap it to rescue it from the floor. The cat was never involved.

Cat is eyeing the bar

Blob Glutton

liquid · pixel art

Every bit it swallows is progress. Drag or flick bits into its mouth to feed it faster. It burps at every quarter and gets rounder.

Peckish

Everything is drawn live on canvas, with no images. Progress is simulated: an uneven pace, the odd stall and a slow last ten percent.

Reduced-motion settings calm every loader down.
