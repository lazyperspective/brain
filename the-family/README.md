# The Family

Five drawings from Mario Puzo's novel *The Godfather* (1969), each in its own medium, each made entirely in
JavaScript when the page opens. Original interpretations of the novel, not of any film.

| # | Drawing | Medium |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | **A Favour on the Wedding Day** | Charcoal and white chalk, tenebrist |
| 2 | **The Fruit Stand** | Gouache, 1940s magazine illustration |
| 3 | **The Thunderbolt** | Transparent watercolour over pencil |
| 4 | **I Do Renounce Him** | Gothic stained glass |
| 5 | **The Feast of San Rocco** | Sepia pen-and-ink etching with gold wash, a 4320 × 1350 panorama |

Drawings 1–4 are a carousel joined by one string of lights (`js/shared/lights.js`): the wedding's party lights, the
Christmas lights over Ninth Avenue, a festa garland of paper lanterns in Sicily, and a chain of glass beads that ends
as a ruby in the baptism window. Each drawing paints it in its own medium; the shared curve makes it meet exactly at
every seam, so laid side by side the four become one continuous line. Drawing 5 is a single image cut into four
seamless slides. `?export` renders any piece full-bleed at its design size for exporting.
