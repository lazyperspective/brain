curve of the world, the planet, the sun and its planets, and the galaxy — which turns out to be the snail's shell, and we
are back where we began. The score must LOOP SEAMLESSLY: the last sample flows into the first (no fade, no silence; the
reverb tail of the end must sound under the start). Mood: wonder, tenderness, awe — a music box that becomes the cosmos
and becomes a music box again. Gentle, about −16 LUFS integrated, no harsh hits.
