# The Hollow — Fable Saga part V — cue sheet (40.0 s, 30 fps)

Picks up on part IV's last picture: dawn, the healed cloud sea, the hero floating limp and dim, cracked, the broken hilt in
its hand, four faint orb sparks around it. The picture is cut to these times and the score lands on them to the frame
(±1/30 s). HIT = a full impact in picture and music. Caption times are fixed. Never more than ~3 full-frame flashes in
any second; an impact frame is ONE frame. THIS SHEET IS LOCKED: never move a time.

The villain: THE HOLLOW. The ash of both brothers pours into the hero's own shadow and the shadow stands up: the hero's
exact size and silhouette, made of void, with one magenta eye (the Unmaker's) and one crimson eye (the Sunderer's), a
cracked half-mask of porcelain. It fights with the hero's own moves and copies its forms, inverted. For the first time the
hero fights something its own size and speed: this is a DUEL, blade on blade, not a giant.

The surreal idea: the sky is glass. Every heavy blow cracks it, and behind each shard is a different world. The fight
falls and climbs through them.
