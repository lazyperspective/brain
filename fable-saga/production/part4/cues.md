# The Sunderer — Fable Saga part IV — cue sheet (180.0 s, 30 fps)

Picks up on the last frame of part III (the Unmaker's mask shard sinking into the clouds at dawn, its magenta eye open).
The picture is cut to these times and the score lands its hits on them (to the frame, ±1/30 s). HIT = a full orchestral
impact. Captions are fixed. Never more than ~3 full-frame flashes in any second; an impact frame is ONE frame.
