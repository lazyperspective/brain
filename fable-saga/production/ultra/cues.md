# ultrathink — a 15-second Fable short (15.000 s, 30 fps)

The hero (plain coral, as Part IV left it) faces a towering wall that is a maze of glowing code blocks. Its normal swing
does nothing. It closes its eyes, `ultrathink` types out, a drop of mustard lands on the blade, time stops, and a hundred
ghost-copies of the hero search the maze at once until one golden path lights up. One clean cut along that path, and the
whole wall falls. Score: a playful but heroic cue (a quick Fable-hero-theme quote, D–A–B–A–D'), not the Part IV dread.
